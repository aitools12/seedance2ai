What is Seedance2AI

Seedance2AI is a web-based AI video generation platform centered around Seedance-powered workflows. It helps users create videos from text prompts or turn still images into dynamic clips with cinematic motion, multi-shot storytelling, and downloadable output.

Why use it

Most AI video tools feel fragmented or overly technical. Seedance2AI makes the workflow simpler by combining prompt-driven creation, reference uploads, output settings, and downloadable short-form video generation in one interface.

How it works

  1. Add a prompt or upload a reference image.
  2. Choose text-to-video or image-to-video settings.
  3. Generate and download a short cinematic clip.
