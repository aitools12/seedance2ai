Workflows

How Seedance 2.0 fits real production workflows

The strongest use of Seedance 2.0 is not just “generate a cool clip.” It is about speeding up concepting, campaign testing, and visual output for short-form channels without needing a heavy video stack.

  1. Start with a direct prompt or a reference image. Use descriptive motion, camera, subject, and lighting language instead of vague slogans.
  2. Choose the right generation path. Text-to-video is better for ideation. Image-to-video is better when art direction or product consistency matters.
  3. Review, refine, and export. Use the official showcase and output examples to calibrate style before you spend more credits on production runs.
